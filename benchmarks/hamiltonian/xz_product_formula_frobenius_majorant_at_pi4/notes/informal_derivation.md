# XZ product-formula Frobenius majorant at t = pi/4

The kernel-checked instance is Pauli X,Z on C^2 at t = pi/4. The product-formula
error has positive entry modulus dominated by the Frobenius mass of the difference.

This is not a general operator-norm first-order Lie-Trotter theorem with universal
constants, and it is not an entrywise-only certificate being relabeled as operator-norm
closure.
