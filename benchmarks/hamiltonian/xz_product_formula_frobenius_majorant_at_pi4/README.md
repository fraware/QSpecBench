# XZ product-formula Frobenius majorant at pi/4

## Claim

For Pauli X and Z on C^2 at t = pi/4, the first-order product-formula error has positive entry modulus dominated by the Frobenius mass of the difference. This is a concrete matrix-norm instance, not a general operator-norm Lie-Trotter theorem.
